# Project brief

You are building a data platform for a company that sells freight analytics to shippers moving goods by air and ocean. Ops users need live shipment status. Executives want spend, carrier reliability and savings.

You work alone in week 1. In session 3 each student gets one of seven shipper profiles, each with its own service-level targets; three students share each profile. In session 4 those three form a team, compare their designs and agree on one.

Due: Tuesday 15 December, in your team repo.
